Privacy Policy

Timeshare Calculators is designed to work without an account and to keep the information you enter on your device. This policy explains the limited information used by the app and website.

In brief: We do not receive your calculator entries, AI questions or responses, company searches, customer notes, or Why training answers.

Information you enter in the app

Values entered into the Finance, Vacation, and Quick calculators—such as purchase prices, down payments, loan terms, interest rates, vacation costs, and inflation assumptions—remain on your device. We do not receive or store those values.

Apple Intelligence

When you use Timeshare AI, Ask AI About This Company, or AI Comprehensive Analysis, responses are generated using Apple Intelligence on your device. Your questions and generated responses are not sent to us and are not included in our analytics. Apple Intelligence availability depends on compatible hardware, software, language, and regional support.

App usage analytics

We use Amplitude to understand which parts of the app are useful and to improve reliability. The app sends fixed interaction names, such as when a screen or feature is opened, along with limited technical context such as device type, operating system, and app version. Amplitude may assign a pseudonymous installation or device identifier so events can be counted over time.

We configure analytics not to collect IP address, city, designated market area, region, device vendor identifier, or carrier. Analytics events do not contain calculator values, company searches, AI prompts or responses, customer information, notes, or other text you enter. We do not use app analytics for advertising.

Offline use

The calculators, Why training, and stored company information can be used without an internet connection. Analytics can be transmitted only when the device is online. Some system and Apple Intelligence capabilities may have their own availability requirements.

No account or login

The app does not require an account or login, and we do not maintain user profiles.

This website

This website does not use advertising trackers, analytics cookies, contact forms, or user accounts. Like other websites, its hosting and security providers may process standard technical request information, such as IP address, browser information, requested page, and time of access, to deliver and protect the site.

Links to other services

The app and website may link to the App Store, official company websites, or other external services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app or its services change. The effective date at the top identifies the latest revision.

Contact

For privacy questions, email support@timesharecalculators.com.
