Explore answers to buyer questions

Search in everyday language, choose a suggested buyer question, and review an on-device Apple Intelligence response. In this example, the app explores why maintenance fees may increase and what to consider when estimating future costs.

AI-generated answers can make mistakes. Verify important information.

Company research

Find related brands, then open the overview

Search more than 60 timeshare companies and see affiliated brands and parent-company relationships. Open an entry for structured information, exchange affiliation, booking flexibility, and a link to the company’s official website.

Why training

Understand the situation behind the decision

Use neutral questions to uncover what changed, what is not working about the customer’s current vacations, and what progress they hope to make. The focus is their situation—not age, income, family size, or other demographics—so you can recognize possible needs and whether vacation ownership may genuinely fit.

Private by design

Your presentation stays with you

Calculator inputs stay on your device. Purchase prices, vacation costs, and other values are not sent to us.

AI runs through Apple Intelligence on your device. Questions and responses are not included in app analytics.

The core tools work offline. Calculators, training content, and stored company information remain available without an internet connection.
